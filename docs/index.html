Hisscore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Hisscore is a Snake game. Almost everything it does happens on your device. The one exception is the global leaderboards, which send your score and a display name to a server. This page explains exactly what is sent, when, and to whom.

What leaves your device

When you finish a game with a score above zero, Hisscore automatically submits that run to the global leaderboards. This happens at the end of every run, without asking you first. What is sent is:

  • Your display name. If you never choose one, the app generates a name for you from your anonymous ID; you can change it at any time on the STATS tab.
  • Your score for that run, and which board it belongs to (the day's Daily Challenge, or the all-time board for that mode).
  • A timestamp, set by the server when the entry is written.
  • An anonymous player ID, described below, which the entry is stored under.

Nothing else is transmitted. No email address, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifier, and no account you hold anywhere else.

The anonymous player ID

To keep one leaderboard row per player rather than one per run, the app signs in to Firebase Anonymous Authentication the first time it needs the leaderboard. This creates a random identifier that is not tied to your name, email, phone number, or Google account, and is not shared with other apps. It is stored on your device and by Google, and it persists until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Who processes it

The leaderboards run on Google Firebase — Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Data sent to the leaderboard is stored on Google's servers and handled under Firebase's privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy. Hisscore has no other backend.

There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, top-5 local leaderboard, cumulative stats, daily-challenge streak, daily quests, XP and level, unlocked themes and skins, your chosen settings, and a short local crash log are stored only in this app's own storage on your device, using the platform's standard local-storage APIs. None of that leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Crash reports stay on your device. The app is built with no crash reporting service configured, so crashes are written to that local log and are not sent anywhere.

Permissions

The release build requests one runtime permission: POST_NOTIFICATIONS, used solely to schedule a local reminder for your next Daily Challenge. That reminder is scheduled and delivered entirely on-device — no notification content or token is sent anywhere. The app also declares network access, which is used only for the leaderboards described above.

If you would rather not appear on the leaderboards

Score submission currently has no in-app opt-out. Until one exists, you can keep your runs off the global boards by playing with the device offline — the app works fully offline and a failed submission is ignored. You can also change your display name at any time on the STATS tab.

To have a leaderboard entry removed, email the address below with the display name shown on the board and roughly when it was set, and it will be deleted.

Children's privacy

The game is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only thing a player can enter is a display name, which appears publicly on the leaderboards — please do not use a real name. If you believe a child has submitted identifying information as a display name, email the address below and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If what the app sends ever changes, this page will be updated with a new "last updated" date above.
